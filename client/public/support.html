Common questions

  • A tournament is missing or wrong. Official TFAA, Texas ASA and TSAA events come from each association's public schedule and update every few hours. Always confirm details with the host club. If one is still wrong after a day, email me.
  • How do I add a tournament? Tournaments tab → + Add a tournament. Archer-added events are labeled "Added by archer" and show who added them.
  • How do friends work? Account → Friends shows your friend code. Share it, or enter a friend's code. When you mark a shoot Going you choose who sees it: your friends, everyone in the app, or just you.
  • Reporting a listing, tournament or person. Tap the ••• or Report button on it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block someone from the same menu.
  • Ad-free and tips. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel the ad-free subscription, open your phone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions (iPhone) or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (Android). To restore it on a new phone, use Restore purchases on the Go ad-free screen.
  • Deleting your account. Account → Delete account removes your profile, friends, shared Going list, tournaments you added, listings, photos and messages right away.
