Privacy Policy

Archery in Texas app and the Texas Archery Calendar website · Last updated October 3, 2026

Archery in Texas shows TFAA, Texas ASA and TSAA tournament schedules, tournaments added by archers, and a marketplace for used archery gear. You can browse the schedule without an account. An account is only needed to add friends, share the shoots you're going to, add tournaments, post listings or send messages.

What stays on your phone

  • Your Going list and the last schedule the app downloaded are saved on your device. A tournament you mark Going is only sent to us if you choose to share it (see below); "Just me" keeps it on your phone.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Calendar: the app only adds an event when you tap "Add to Calendar." It doesn't read your calendar.

Accounts

  • Sign-in: with Sign in with Apple or your email address. Your email is used only to sign you in and is never shown to other people. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only see a relay address.
  • Public profile: the name, optional city and optional archery class you choose, and when you joined, are shown to other people.
  • Friends: each account has a friend code. Your friend list and requests are visible only to you and the friends involved. Either of you can remove a friendship at any time, and blocking someone removes it too. If you turn on "Let other archers find me by name," signed-in archers can find your name, city and class by searching; it's your choice, and you can turn it off anytime in Edit profile.
  • Shared Going: when you mark a tournament Going you choose who can see it. "My friends" shows your name and class on that tournament to your friends; "Everyone in the app" shows them to any signed-in archer. You can change or stop sharing at any time.
  • Tournaments you add are public, with your name shown as the person who added them, until you delete them. Other archers can report them.
  • Listings: the photos, descriptions and prices you post are public until you delete them or mark them sold.
  • Messages: stored so you and the other person can read them. We look at messages only when one is reported, to keep the marketplace safe.
  • Reports and blocks you make are kept to enforce the marketplace rules.
  • This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Deleting your account: Account → Delete account permanently removes your profile, friends, shared Going list, tournaments you added, listings, photos and messages right away.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only and don't track you across other apps or websites. To show ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, IP address, approximate location from that IP, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad-free subscription

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, which uses an anonymous app ID and your purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Schedule data

Tournament information comes from the public schedules of TFAA, Texas ASA and TSAA, and from tournaments added by archers in the app, which are labeled "Added by archers." Always confirm details with the host club.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

We don't sell your data

We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising, and we don't run our own analytics.
