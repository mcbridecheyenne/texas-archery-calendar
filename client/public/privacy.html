Privacy Policy

Archery in the USA app (formerly Archery in Texas) and website · Last updated October 9, 2026

Archery in the USA shows archery tournament schedules from across the country (including TFAA, Texas ASA and TSAA in Texas), tournaments added by archers, and a marketplace for used archery gear. You can browse the schedule without an account. An account is only needed to add friends, share the shoots you're going to, add tournaments, post listings or send messages.

What stays on your phone

  • Your Going list and the last schedule the app downloaded are saved on your device. If you aren't signed in, your Going list stays only on your phone.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Calendar: the app only adds an event when you tap "Add to Calendar." It doesn't read your calendar.

Accounts

  • Sign-in: with Sign in with Apple. Your email is used only to sign you in and is never shown to other people. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only see a relay address.
  • Profile: the name, optional city and optional archery class you choose, and when you joined, are shown to other signed-in archers. People who aren't signed in can't see profiles. Your home state and friend code are private: only you can see them.
  • Age: when you make an account we ask your birth month and year to check that you're 13 or older. We don't save your birthday, only the date you confirmed your age.
  • Friends: each account has a friend code. Your friend list and requests are visible only to you and the friends involved. Either of you can remove a friendship at any time, and blocking someone removes it too. If you turn on "Let other archers find me by name," signed-in archers can find your name, city and class by searching; it's your choice, and you can turn it off anytime in Edit profile.
  • Going list on your account: when you're signed in, every tournament you mark Going is also saved to your account (name, date and who can see it), so your list comes back on a new phone. You choose who can see each one: "Just me" is saved to your account but seen only by you; "My friends" shows your name and class on that tournament to your friends; "Everyone in the app" shows them to any signed-in archer. You can change who sees it, or remove it, at any time.
  • Tournaments you add are public, with your name shown as the person who added them, until you delete them. Other archers can report them.
  • Listings: the photos, descriptions and prices you post are public until you delete them or mark them sold.
  • Messages: stored so you and the other person can read them. We look at messages only when one is reported, to keep the marketplace safe.
  • Reports and blocks you make are kept to enforce the marketplace rules.
  • This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Deleting your account: Account → Delete account permanently removes your profile, friends, Going list, tournaments you added, listings, photos and messages right away. If you can't use the app, you can ask us to delete your account instead.

Location

  • The app asks for your approximate location, only while you're using it. It's never tracked in the background.
  • Near me: used on your phone to show tournaments within driving distance. It isn't sent to us.
  • Marketplace: used to show gear you can pick up nearby. When you post a listing, its pickup spot is saved rounded to about 3 miles.
  • If you don't allow location, the app still works; those features are just hidden.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app saves your phone's notification code to your account so we can tell you about new messages and friend requests. The notification (the sender's name and the start of a message) is delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google. Turn notifications off anytime in your phone's Settings. Signing out or deleting your account removes the code.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and ad cards in the tournament list. We request non-personalized ads only and don't track you across other apps or websites. To show ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, IP address, approximate location from that IP, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Hotel and flight links

A tournament's details can show "Find hotels" (through Stay22) and "Find flights" (through Expedia) buttons. They open those companies' websites with the shoot's town and dates filled in. We don't send them your name, account or location. If you book, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Ad-free subscription

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, which uses an anonymous app ID and your purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Featured shoots

"Feature this shoot" is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play, checked with RevenueCat the same way as the ad-free subscription. The featured card shows the name you enter as the promoter, the shoot's public details, and how many times it was shown and opened. We keep a record of who bought each feature (your account) in case of abuse. Featured shoots are labeled, and you can hide them in the Account tab at no cost.

Schedule data

Tournament information comes from public schedules (such as TFAA, Texas ASA, TSAA, S3DA and World Archery), from basic facts (name, dates and place) about other major events with a link to the organizer's page, from shoots host clubs send us, and from tournaments added by archers in the app, which are labeled "Added by archers." We aren't affiliated with or endorsed by these organizations. Always confirm details with the host club.

Children

The app is for people 13 and older. Accounts can't be made by anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

We don't sell your data

We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising, and we don't run our own analytics.
