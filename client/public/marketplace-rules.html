Marketplace Rules

Archery in the USA (formerly Archery in Texas) · Last updated October 4, 2026

The marketplace is a place for archers to buy, sell and trade used archery gear with each other, for local pickup. By making an account, posting or messaging, you agree to these rules.

13 and older

You must be 13 or older to make an account. Accounts found to belong to someone under 13 are deleted.

Archery gear only

  • Bows, crossbows, arrows and shafts, sights, rests, releases, stabilizers, cases, quivers, targets, points and broadheads, and archery apparel.
  • Not allowed: firearms, ammunition, firearm parts or accessories, knives other than field-dressing tools, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or vapes, stolen items, counterfeits, animals or animal parts, and anything illegal where you or the buyer live.

Be honest

  • Use your own photos, describe the condition accurately, and include draw length, draw weight and hand where it applies.
  • Only list gear you own and have on hand. Mark it sold when it's gone.

Zero tolerance

There is no tolerance for harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, spam or scams. We review every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the people who post it are banned.

Stay safe

  • Meet in a public place. An upcoming tournament is ideal; use "Hand off at a shoot" on your listing.
  • Inspect gear before you pay. Never pay with gift cards, wire transfers or crypto, and never pay a stranger before you see the item.
  • Use Report and Block on any listing or conversation that seems wrong.

Featured shoots

Anyone signed in can pay to feature an upcoming shoot at the top of a state's list or the All states list for 7, 14 or 30 days. It's labeled "Featured · promoted by" and the name of the person who paid, and it doesn't change the shoot's details or mean an organizer or association endorses it. Featured shoots must follow these rules; one that doesn't can be removed, and a feature ends when the shoot is over. Anyone can turn featured shoots off for free: in the app, Account → Show featured shoots; on the website, "Hide featured shoots".

Our role

Archery in the USA helps people find each other. We don't own, inspect or guarantee items, and we aren't part of any sale. Buyers and sellers are responsible for their own deals and for following the law, including any age requirements for buying bows or broadheads.

Questions? Use the support link on the app's App Store or Google Play page.
