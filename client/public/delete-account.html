Delete your Archery in the USA account

Archery in the USA app, by Cheyenne McBride

Fastest: in the app

Open the app, go to Account → Delete account, and confirm. Your account and everything in it are removed right away.

Without the app

If you've deleted the app or can't sign in, email us and we'll delete your account for you:

  1. Send an email to mcbridecheyenne81@icloud.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Include the name on your profile and the email you signed in with. If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", you can find that address on your iPhone in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Archery in the USA.
  3. We'll confirm by email, delete the account within 7 days, and let you know when it's done.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in and profile (name, city, archery class, home state, friend code)
  • Your friends and friend requests, and your Going list
  • Tournaments you added, your marketplace listings and their photos
  • Your messages and conversations, and reports you filed
  • The phone notification codes linked to your account

We don't keep any of this after your account is deleted. Copies in our database provider's routine backups are erased as those backups expire, within 30 days.

Not covered

Things saved only on your phone (the downloaded schedule and reminders) are removed when you delete the app. Ad-free subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so cancel them in your phone's subscription settings.
