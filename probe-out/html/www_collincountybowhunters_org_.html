About the Club

The Collin County Bow Hunters Association is a non-profit organization that was founded in 1985. The association is run by a group of dedicated volunteers who share a love of archery and a desire to educate and inspire others. In addition to promoting archery and bow hunting, the Collin County Bow Hunters Association is also committed to preserving and protecting natural resources and wildlife habitats. The association works closely with state and local conservation agencies to promote responsible hunting practices and to protect the environment. The association welcomes members of all ages and skill levels, from beginners to seasoned archers and hunters. Overall, the Collin County Bow Hunters Association is a vibrant community of archers and hunters who share a passion for archery and a commitment to promoting ethical and responsible hunting practices. If you are interested learning more about the association, Contact Us.

Our Mission

To create a setting for honing bowhunting skills in authentic hunting scenarios, while inspiring enthusiasm for archery through community engagement.
Membership

Our Archery Range

The club has a 70-acre range northeast of Princeton near Verona, Texas. The range has open hilly trails with woody areas for shooting. We offer a static practice range and 3D practice range for all members. The static range has practice blob targets from 10 to 60 yards, and the 3D range has 30 + practice 3D targets setup throughout the 70-acre range.
The range is unstaffed, but available year-round, during daylight hours for current members.

Practice with Confidence

We can develop confidence when hunting with our consistent practice in real hunting scenarios. We learn about our capabilities and limits which help each of us be more ethical hunters and demonstrate our respect for the animals we hunt utilizing archery equipment.  The range is set up to maintain shooting safety when the club’s range rules are followed, and practical shooting courtesies are practiced.

Realistic 3D Target Shooting

Arrows and archery equipment do what they are designed and set up to do.  The sport of archery is interesting and competitive in indoor environments.  However, add the elements of a live animal and the outdoor environment, you find bowhunting very interesting and competitive in so many other ways. With the realistic 3D target shooting, the targets have depth and angles, vitals change and must be considered.  Trees, limbs, twigs etc must be considered, the wind speed and direction can further complicate the shot.  Just like hitting a golf ball on a indoor range can be challenging and fun, moving this to hitting a gold ball on a golf course is more interesting and competitive.

Protecting The Range Wildlife

There are live animals on our range; white-tailed deer, hogs, rabbits, squirrels, etc. Hunting or shooting these animals on the range is strictly prohibited and individuals will lose their membership rights if they do.

Range Rules

During the annual membership application process, members will read, understand, sign and follow the Collin County Bowhunters Association Range Rules to maintain membership privileges in the club.
