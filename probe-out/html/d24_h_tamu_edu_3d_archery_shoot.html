3D Archery Shoot

IN ORDER TO PARTICIPATE, 4-H MEMBER MUST HAVE THEIR PERMISSION SLIP SIGNED BY A CERTIFIED TEXAS 4-H ARCHERY COACH, WHO AGREES THAT THEY ARE NOT A SAFETY RISK.

3D Archery Shoot

Date:   June 21, 2025

Location:    South Plains Archery Club

6596 FM 835   Lubbock, TX

Time:   Registration begins at 7:30 a.m.

                                                                  Shooting Begins at 8:30 a.m.

Entry Fee:      $15 on 4-H Online

 $5 per shooter per equipment class to Pay South Plains Archery Club

4-H online:    Opens: May 27, 2025

                     Closes: June 13, 2025
