Who is Fort Grard

Fort Grard was est. June of 2014. The building was opened Oct 31, 2015. We as the Grard family built Fort Grard from the ground up. We are family owned and operated and strive to make archery affordable for everyone. We want everyone to feel at home when they come into our store. When you walk through our doors you're family.

Fort Grard seeks to create and build a long lasting, and honest relationship with our customers.  We strive to grow our business with the same integrity and honesty we seek to find in our employees.  We look to give quality and value in our products and services to all customers that walk through our door.
