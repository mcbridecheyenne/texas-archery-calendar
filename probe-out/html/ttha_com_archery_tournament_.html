ARCHERY TOURNAMENT

EVENT DETAILS

Enter your deer from last season into the annual archery contest for a chance to win great prizes and statewide bragging rights. The contest is free to all members of the Texas Trophy Hunters Association. Not a member? That’s ok – you can join online or at the Extravaganza. There are multiple contest categories for rifle, archery, low-fence, high-fence, adult, youth & more. See official rules for more details or contact TTHA today.

OUR MISSION

TTHA is the “Voice of Texas Hunting”, and strives to unite all segments of the hunting community to promote, protect, and preserve Texas’ wildlife resources and hunting heritage for our future generations.
