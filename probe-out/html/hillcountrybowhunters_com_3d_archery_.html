3D Archery Tournaments

To raise funds for our Youth Outdoor Awards Program, HCB hosts eight (8) monthly 3D Archery tournaments each year between February and September. We also host several ASA ("Archery Shooters Association") qualifying tournament, tournaments by the Traditional Bowhunters of Texas ("TBoT") and one tournament by the Williamson County 4H Shooting Sports Club.  All proceeds exceeding the operating costs of the organization are directed to the Outdoor Awards Program.

Tournaments are open to the public and we welcome shooters of all ages and abilities. Please refer to the schedule and additional information below.
