    Lemon Grove Rod & Gun Club Women’s Shooting Clinic

    Overview:
    Join us for our Women’s Pistol, Rifle, and Shotgun Shooting Clinic! These clinics are designed as an introduction to shooting sports, providing a supportive environment for women to learn about firearms safety and handling.

    Important Information:

    • Eligibility: Open to women 16 and older. 
    • Firearms: Participants will learn with .22 caliber rifles, handguns, and 12/20-gauge pump shotguns. Larger-caliber centerfire handguns may also be available. No outside firearms are allowed.
    • Instruction: Each participant will receive personalized, one-on-one instruction from experienced, NRA Certified Instructors. Safety briefings will be conducted before the shooting begins.

    Registration Details:

    • Payment: Full payment is required upon registration. No refunds or transfers for cancellations. Only one person can register at a time; if registering for someone else, please provide their details.
    • Check-In: Arrive between 7:15 a.m. and 7:45 a.m. for check-in and safety equipment. A mandatory safety class starts at 8:00 a.m. sharp. Late arrivals after 7:45 a.m. will not be admitted.
    • Refunds: There are no refunds nor are walk-ins accepted. If you cancel, we cannot transfer you to another clinic. Please ensure you can attend before you register and pay.

    What to Wear:

    • Comfortable tennis shoes (no open-toe shoes or flip-flops).
    • Long pants or jeans (Capris are acceptable, no shorts).
    • No low-cut or V-neck tops. (No tank tops allowed) 
    • A baseball hat or visor is recommended for sun protection and flying brass.

    Logistics:

    • The clinic will end around noon. Expect to walk on dirt and uneven gravel, so dress appropriately. We will shoot in the rain. 
    • If attending with friends, arrive together, and we will do our best to place you in the same squad (max up to 16 per squad, but we have to keep our squads even if we have fewer participants).
    • A reminder email will be sent 7 days before the event with specific details. Check your spam for the email.

    Waitlist Information:

    • If you're on the waitlist, we will contact you when a spot opens. Please respond quickly to secure your place. If you cannot attend, please kindly respond so we can contact the next participant. 
    • Our waitlist is also known as the standby list for the day of the event. We will notify you directly with further information. Please add women@lgrgc.com to your contact to ensure our emails are not sent to spam. 

    Thank You to Our Sponsors:
    A special thanks to the Lemon Grove Rod & Gun Club, San Diego Chapter of Safari Club International, Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation, California Rifle & Pistol Association, and NRA for their generous support, helping us continue to offer our program at an affordable price. 

    We look forward to seeing you at the clinic! For any questions, feel free to reach out.

    For any questions, please contact Robyn at women@lgrgc.com.

    We hold these events on the second Sunday in January, May, and October. All registration is handled through our website. We cannot add anyone via email.

    CCW SKILLS CLINIC

    This is an advanced Clinic for EDC that are training with frequency. This is NOT a beginner Clinic. All stages and scenarios in this module are live fire. There is no Force-on-Force training in this Module.

    This clinic is built specifically for valid EDC Club Members that actually carry everyday, to provide training using practical “real-life” scenario-based stages, and drill-oriented EDC skill building modules, thematically built upon the AVOID EVADE DEFEND concepts of smart EDC. 

    If you are new to CCW and or EDC training, it is required that you enroll in the Beginner Defensive Handgun class on the 2nd Friday of the month with Shoot Master Stan Luhr before you advance to the CCW Skills Clinic. Registration for the DHG Class is required with Stan @ 619 890 5404 or sluhr@ppcexpert.com.

    Pre-registration with the CCW Clinic Shoot Master is required. A current CA, valid CCW license, must be presented to attend CCW SC. The firearm used must be on the license. THE SIG P320 and its variants MAY NOT BE USED IN THIS CLINIC. Firearm and license check are mandatory, prestart to participate. An appropriate sturdy gun belt with IWB holster, preferably Kydex and two magazines will be needed. All draw work is from concealed Appendix Inside Waist Band. No other carry positions are allowed. Round count variable. Estimate one hundred plus per event.

    Cost per event per member $20. Non-member guests, including prospects, not allowed. Not open to the public.

    New participant orientation, firearm and license check begins at 1:15 pm SHARP to align safety protocols and assess dry fire draw skill and safe gun handling skill from concealed, culminating in an abbreviated basic marksmanship live fire from concealed. You must pass the Basic Standard timed live fire draw to first shot at 3 yards, USPSA A Zone hit, in less than 1.80 seconds to qualify for the Clinic. The remaining time of the first 30 minutes will be to set the range for that specific clinics course(s) of fire, for the Module. Prior club member participants begin the clinic thereafter, with a Safety Brief at 1:45 pm.

    If you are late for the New Participant Orientation or to the1:45 Safety Brief or arrive without being pre-registered for the Clinic you will be unable to participate.

     The Shoot Master for CCW Skills Clinic; Blake Beckcom, is reachable via email at blknatr@yahoo.com and by phone at 619 806 8127.

    The day of class you need to bring:
    DO NOT BRING ANY LIVE AMMUNITION INTO THE CLASSROOM! 

    1. Pen/Pencil to write with. Highlighters are a good idea but not required.
    2. Lunch/Snacks/Non-alcoholic beverages to get you through the full

    3. Eye and ear protection.

    4. Range appropriate attire (baseball cap/crew collar shirt (no plunging necklines), closed toe shoes). Clothing appropriate for the weather that day.

    INSTRUCTOR QUALIFICATION STANDARDS - Each instructor candidate must pass the following test:

    Using any rifle, with any optics, hit 4.5" target at 25 yards. You will fire 10 shots each from sitting, prone, standing unsupported. You must hit the target a minimum of 24 times. You will be allowed up to 2 additional attempts if necessary.

    Some things that you could do to help make the class easier for you:
    1. Go touch, handle, fondle, caress as many different types of

    2. Shoot as many different types of these guns as possible.
    3. Get as much range time in as you can, either at our range or anywhere else and practice with your rifle getting 4 inch or smaller groups at 25 yards.

  • PERSONAL / FAMILY DEFENSIVE DECSION MAKING SCENARIOS

    • 11/11/2026
    • 14:00 - 18:00
    • LGRGC - TRANING CLASSROOM
    Register

    Personal / Family Defense Decision Making Scenarios - Test your decision making skills in realistic situations involving other students.

    Members-$30 Individual/$50 Family  Non-members: $60 Individual/$100 Family.

    When signing up each adult needs to be registered separately, children (17 and under) do not need to be registered. Payment will be collected via cash or Zelle upon arrival.

    Contact Dave Reed at davereed2010@gmail.com with any questions.

    Personal defense preparation is far more than going to the range and shooting at paper targets. This seminar will put you, and any family members you bring with you, in realistic scenarios where you will have to decide what your best option is, whether you carry a gun or not. Can I walk away? Is pepper spray an option? If I must use my gun what actions should my family take? If I'm not alone what are each individual's responsibilities in a defensive situation? If I don't carry a gun what options do I have?

    Family guidelines for the class - If you have kids, parents, physically/mentally challenged people in your daily life you need to be able to protect them, bad things can happen anywhere at anytime. Including them in the class brings a whole new set of concerns to your response to a critical situation. All ages and abilities are welcome. If they get too disruptive I may ask you to take them outside long enough to calm them down, but you will still get a huge benefit from the class. During scenarios where you are the citizen defender you will have to deal with it, just like you would in a real life scenario. I think that makes the training even more effective for your day to day life, as these are things you will have to work around to stay safe.

    Every family member residing in the same household is welcome at the family price. Kids need to be able to be fairly quiet for 20-30 minutes at a time. Obviously if someone needs to step away to take care of the little ones that is perfectly acceptable.

    We have a lift for wheelchairs to get into the classroom. It will be a challenge moving around in the parking area due to gravel, I will assist you in any way possible. 

    We will be using a combination of red guns. shot indication laser pistols, inert pepper spray (water), training medical supplies and other tools during decision making scenarios run against other students. None of our training aids fire any type of projectile, so you don't need to worry about anyone getting hurt from that. 

    DUE TO SAFETY CONCERNS ABSOLUTELY NO SELF DEFENSE TOOLS, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO KNIVES, GUNS, PEPPER SPRAY, TASERS, STUN GUNS, KUBATONS, ETC. ALLOWED IN THE VICINITY OF THE CLASS. YOU WILL BE PATTED DOWN BY A MINIMUM OF TWO PEOPLE PRIOR TO PARTICIPATION IN THIS CLASS

    This is a serious class based on realistic real world scenarios, not a first person shooter game trying to see who can shoot first, fastest or most number of rounds. The best outcome you can hope for is that you do not have to draw your training firearm at all.

    Personal / Family Defense Decision Making Scenarios - Test your decision making skills in realistic situations involving other students.

    Members-$30 Individual/$50 Family  Non-members: $60 Individual/$100 Family.

    When signing up each adult needs to be registered separately, children (17 and under) do not need to be registered. Payment will be collected via cash or Zelle upon arrival.

    Contact Dave Reed at davereed2010@gmail.com with any questions.

    Personal defense preparation is far more than going to the range and shooting at paper targets. This seminar will put you, and any family members you bring with you, in realistic scenarios where you will have to decide what your best option is, whether you carry a gun or not. Can I walk away? Is pepper spray an option? If I must use my gun what actions should my family take? If I'm not alone what are each individual's responsibilities in a defensive situation? If I don't carry a gun what options do I have?

    Family guidelines for the class - If you have kids, parents, physically/mentally challenged people in your daily life you need to be able to protect them, bad things can happen anywhere at anytime. Including them in the class brings a whole new set of concerns to your response to a critical situation. All ages and abilities are welcome. If they get too disruptive I may ask you to take them outside long enough to calm them down, but you will still get a huge benefit from the class. During scenarios where you are the citizen defender you will have to deal with it, just like you would in a real life scenario. I think that makes the training even more effective for your day to day life, as these are things you will have to work around to stay safe.

    Every family member residing in the same household is welcome at the family price. Kids need to be able to be fairly quiet for 20-30 minutes at a time. Obviously if someone needs to step away to take care of the little ones that is perfectly acceptable.

    We have a lift for wheelchairs to get into the classroom. It will be a challenge moving around in the parking area due to gravel, I will assist you in any way possible. 

    We will be using a combination of red guns. shot indication laser pistols, inert pepper spray (water), training medical supplies and other tools during decision making scenarios run against other students. None of our training aids fire any type of projectile, so you don't need to worry about anyone getting hurt from that. 

    DUE TO SAFETY CONCERNS ABSOLUTELY NO SELF DEFENSE TOOLS, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO KNIVES, GUNS, PEPPER SPRAY, TASERS, STUN GUNS, KUBATONS, ETC. ALLOWED IN THE VICINITY OF THE CLASS. YOU WILL BE PATTED DOWN BY A MINIMUM OF TWO PEOPLE PRIOR TO PARTICIPATION IN THIS CLASS

    This is a serious class based on realistic real world scenarios, not a first person shooter game trying to see who can shoot first, fastest or most number of rounds. The best outcome you can hope for is that you do not have to draw your training firearm at all.
