About Us

Archery Lessons and Team

In 2007, Coach Amy and Coach Terry were looking for a family activity and remembered how much we both enjoyed archery as a children.  We began shooting as a family and competing in tournaments. In 2014 we started Tejas JOAD Archery to bring other archers and familes along on our great adventure.

Competitive Archery Team

40 plus members strong, Tejas JOAD Archers compete in local, state and national archery tournaments through USA Archery, National Field Archery Association, ASA, and National Archery in the Schools
