Welcome to Hog Heaven

The Country Club of Dripping Springs

Hog Heaven is a sporting club, outdoor education facility, event venue, and nature preserve set against the backdrop of the Texas Hill Country. Spanning over 120 acres and anchored by a 15-acre stocked lake, sporting clays, pistol, archery, and a full calendar of social events are all here, and all only thirty minutes from Austin.

Hog Heaven Sporting Club is a place to learn new skills, master your craft, and gather in community. But most of all, for those of us who feel the outdoors as part of our soul and our identity, this is a little slice of heaven, right in your own backyard.

The Property

All of your outdoor pursuits in one place

Shotgun

Sporting clays course wrapping around the lake, plus skeet, trap, and 5-stand.

  • 12 sporting clays stations
  • 4 trap and skeet fields
  • 5 station super sporting course
  • Two 5-stands

Pistol & Carbine

Nine outdoor bays with interactive steel and paper targets, and instruction at every level.

  • 9 pistol and carbine bays
  • Interactive steel and paper targets
  • Lessons from working pros

Archery & The Lake

A 3D archery gallery and a spring-fed lake stocked for real fishing.

  • 3D gallery with Texas native and big game targets
  • 15-acre stocked lake, 200+ underwater habitat structures
  • Bass, bluegill, and catfish
