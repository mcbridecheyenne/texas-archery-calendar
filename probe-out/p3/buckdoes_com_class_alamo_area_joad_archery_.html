BUCK & DOE’S JOAD ARCHERY CLUB

Join the Buck & Doe's JOAD Club!

 Ready to take your archery skills to the next level? Our JOAD (Junior Olympic Archery Development) Club is perfect for intermediate to advanced archers who want to grow in a fun, supportive environment. Buck & Doe's JOAD CLUB is where you'll train for local, state, national, and even international competitions through organizations like NFAA, USA Archery, ASA, and S3DA. Our mission is to build a strong team of competitive archers, while teaching goal-setting and life skills that go far beyond the range. Plus, we travel together to tournaments — building camaraderie and unforgettable memories.
To become a member, you'll need an annual JOAD membership ($50) and the Buck & Doe's Membership of your choice. As a bonus, your Buck & Doe’s Membership gives you FREE range access — not only during lessons, but anytime you want to practice!

Our JOAD Club team meets twice a week for team practice with a coach. The Fall 2026 Season our team will be practicing every Wednesday and Friday from 5:45pm - 7:15pm. Come sharpen your aim, make new friends, and be part of an awesome archery community!
Interested in joining the Buck & Doe's JOAD Team?

Call us at 830-980-3637, email info@buckdoes.com, or contact us through our website by clicking the button below to learn more.

Let’s aim higher — together!
