Lonestar 600

  • Sat, Dec 13, 2025 10:00 AM Sun, Dec 14, 2025 5:00 PM
  • Archery HQ (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

We are happy to partner with National Archery Scholarship Tour again. Lonestar 600 is part of a nationwide series of indoor archery tournaments focused on celebrating youth archers and promoting archery as a lifelong sport.

Both youth and adult archers compete in various divisions.

Scholarship awards are paid out to the top 8 youth archers in each category, based on National ranking.

These awards are paid directly to the archers US529 Education Savings Plan or to a qualifying post-secondary school or program, such as technical school, trade school, university, or event culinary school.
